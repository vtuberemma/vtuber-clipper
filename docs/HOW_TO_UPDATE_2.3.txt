HOW TO UPDATE VTuber Clipper to 2.3 BETA  (about 30 seconds)

 1. Close VTuber Clipper.
 2. Unzip this file. You get a folder called "app" and two text files.
 3. Open your VTuberClipper folder (the one with VTuberClipper.exe in it).
 4. Drag the "app" folder from this zip into it and choose "Replace the files in the destination" (Copy and Replace).
    Files that change: streamclip.py, streamclip_app.py, vc20ui.py, vc21ui.py, vc22ui.py, vcmore.py, vcouts.py, vctools.py and CREDITS.txt.
    New files: vc23.py, vc23ui.py, vcclues.py, vcmedia.py, vcnet.py, vcpipe.py, vcsafe.py and vcshare.py.
    The pictures, sounds and emojis are the same as in 2.2.
    Your settings, saved styles, transcripts, music, emotes, your clips and the list of clips you kept are NOT touched - they live in your user folder.
 5. Start VTuberClipper.exe. A "What's new" window shows once.

From 2.3 on you can also update from inside the app: Settings -> About -> "Check for updates", or Tools -> Updates -> "Install a zip I have..." and choose
this zip. The app checks the zip, keeps a copy of every file it replaces and can undo it (Tools -> Updates -> "Undo the last update").
(You need 2.3 or newer for that; to get to 2.3 the first time, use the steps above.)

What is new in 2.3 is listed at the top of README.txt: more clues for finding the moment, a privacy guard with your own private words, clip and flash checks,
reports and texts, download options (only a part of a VOD, a speed limit, a cookies file), snapshots of your settings, an update checker with a safe installer,
50 new things in all - and the list of bugs that were found and fixed. The README also says plainly how far the privacy guard and the finders can be trusted.
Almost everything new is OFF until you switch it on. These are ON because they only protect you: the flash check, the clip check, the disk-space guard,
the privacy guard in "warn" mode, and the extra finding clues (untick "Smart finding (recommended)" for the old way of finding clips).
The older updates (1.6 - 2.2) are included, so you can update straight from 1.5.
If something doesn't start, press Details in the app, or look at %USERPROFILE%\VTuberClipper_log.txt.
