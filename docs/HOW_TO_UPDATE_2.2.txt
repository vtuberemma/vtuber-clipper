HOW TO UPDATE VTuber Clipper to 2.2 BETA  (about 30 seconds)

 1. Close VTuber Clipper.
 2. Unzip this file. You get a folder called "app" and two text files.
 3. Open your VTuberClipper folder (the one with VTuberClipper.exe in it).
 4. Drag the "app" folder from this zip into it and choose "Replace the files in the destination" (Copy and Replace).
    Files that change: streamclip.py, streamclip_app.py, vcpost.py, vcouts.py, vcpresets.py, vc20ui.py, vc21ui.py and CREDITS.txt.
    New files: vcsense.py, vcsmart.py, vcmore.py, vctools.py and vc22ui.py.
    The pictures, sounds and emojis are the same as in 2.1.
    Your settings, saved styles, transcripts, music, emotes, your clips and the list of clips you kept are NOT touched - they live in your user folder.
 5. Start VTuberClipper.exe. A "What's new" window shows once.

What is new in 2.2 is listed at the top of README.txt: Smart finding (it tells gameplay, reactions, talking, stories and singing apart - and the README says
honestly what that does and does not mean), 50 new things (Tools window, Review mode, ready-to-post folder, watch a folder, reaction words, slow-mo,
more caption controls...) and the list of bugs that were found and fixed.
Smart finding is ON by default. To get the old way of finding clips back, untick "Smart finding (recommended)" in the Smart finding tab.
Everything else that is new is OFF until you switch it on. The older updates (1.6 - 2.1) are included, so you can update straight from 1.5.
If something doesn't start, press Details in the app, or look at %USERPROFILE%\VTuberClipper_log.txt.
